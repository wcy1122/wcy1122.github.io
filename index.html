Chengyao Wang 王程钥

I am a Research Scientist at Tencent Hy, working on Multimodal Learning and Agentic AI. I obtained my PhD from the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), advised by Prof. Jiaya Jia and Prof. Bei Yu. Prior to that, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Sun Yat-Sen University.

I am particularly interested in building Multimodal Intelligence that interacts with the physical world and learns continuously from experience. My recent work focuses on multimodal foundation models and embodied AI. Representative work includes LLaMA-VID, Mini-Gemini, MGM-Omni and VLAct.

Always happy to chat about research, open source, life, careers, and investing. Feel free to email me and set up a coffee chat.
